---
name: Kessick XR demo boundary
description: Product strategy separating the calibrated photo compositor and stakeholder concept presentation from any future production XR system.
---

Do not evolve the current calibrated photo compositor into production AR. Treat it as a survey and concept-design tool, and present simulated room viewing or product interaction only as a stakeholder demonstration of a possible future experience. Any eventual production XR work should be a separate system built around approved models, device tracking, anchoring, and explicit platform fallbacks.

**Why:** The current compositor has no camera tracking, anchors, plane detection, occlusion, environmental lighting, or approved 3D product geometry. A polished simulation is useful for communicating business value, but calling it live AR would create false technical and product expectations.

**How to apply:** Keep simulated XR scenes visibly labeled “Concept Demo / Future Experience.” Optimize the meeting flow for customer confidence, reduced design back-and-forth, premium differentiation, conversion, and qualified dealer leads. Do not add WebXR, photogrammetry, or production-AR backend complexity unless the product direction changes explicitly.
